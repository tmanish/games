# Games

A little arcade that runs right in your browser. No downloads, no sign-ups, no apps — open it and play. Works on desktop or phone, and even offline once the page has loaded.

## Start playing

<a href="https://tmanish.github.io/games/">Open the arcade menu</a>, then pick a cabinet:

- `↑` / `↓` — move between games
- `Enter` — start the highlighted game
- Or just tap/click the one you want

If it's hosted online, the menu link will take you straight there.

## 🂡 Blackjack — The Velvet Ace

Classic Vegas blackjack: it's you against the dealer, and the goal is to get closer to **21** than the dealer without going over.

### New to blackjack? The basics

- Number cards are worth their value, face cards (J, Q, K) are worth 10, and an Ace is worth **1 or 11** — whichever helps you most.
- Go over 21 and you **bust** — instant loss.
- An Ace plus a 10-value card on your first two cards is a **blackjack** — the best hand, and it pays extra.

### How a round works

1. **Place your bet.** Click the chips ($5, $25, $100, $500) to stack your wager, then hit **Deal**.
   - **Clear** wipes your bet, **Rebet** repeats your last one.
2. **Play your hand.** You'll see your cards and one of the dealer's. Choose:
   - **Hit** — take another card.
   - **Stand** — keep your total and end your turn.
   - **Double** — double your bet and take exactly one more card.
   - **Split** — if your first two cards match, split them into two separate hands.
3. **The dealer plays,** then you win, lose, or tie.

### What pays what

- Win the hand: pays **even money** (bet $50, win $50).
- Natural blackjack: pays **3 to 2** (bet $50, win $75).
- Tie ("push"): your bet comes back.
- The dealer must stand on 17, so they can't keep drawing forever.

### Good to know

- You start with **$1,000** in chips, and your bank **carries over** every time you come back.
- Run out? The house will stake you another $1,000 so the night keeps going.
- **♪** toggles the casino sound on and off. **↺** resets your bank to $1,000.

### Keyboard shortcuts

`H` hit · `S` stand · `D` double · `P` split · `Enter` deal

## 🎰 Lucky Sevens — Slots

A three-reel slot machine. Line up matching symbols across the center row and you win — the rarer the combo, the bigger the payout.

### How a spin works

1. **Set your bet.** Click the chips to stack a wager. **Max** bets as much as your bank allows, **Clear** resets it.
2. **Pull the handle** — hit the big **SPIN** button (or yank the lever on the side).
3. The three reels stop one by one. Whatever lands on the center line is your result.

### What pays what

Payouts are a multiple of your bet, and only the single best combo on the line counts.

- **7 · 7 · 7** — the jackpot, pays **100×**.
- **Three of a kind** — diamonds 50×, BAR 30×, bells 20×, clovers 14×, grapes 10×, lemons 8×, cherries 6×.
- **Two 7s** anywhere — 10×. **One 7** — 2×.
- **Two cherries** — 3×. **One cherry** — 1×.

So a $25 bet that hits three bells pays $500. Tap the **i** button any time to see the full paytable.

### Keyboard shortcuts

`Enter` or `Space` — spin

## 🎡 Roulette Royale — The Wheel

European single-zero roulette. Place your chips on the table, spin the wheel, and hope the ball lands where you bet.

### New to roulette? The basics

The wheel has numbers **0 to 36**. Each number is red or black, except **0**, which is green. You can bet on a single number, on a color, on odd vs. even, and several other groupings — and you can place as many bets as you like on one spin.

### How a round works

1. **Pick a chip value** ($5, $25, $100, $500) — the selected chip glows.
2. **Click the table** to drop that chip on any bet. Click again to stack more. A token shows the total riding on each spot.
   - **Undo** removes your last chip, **Clear** wipes the table, **Rebet** repeats your last round.
3. **Hit SPIN.** The ball drops, the wheel turns, and the winning number lights up. Winning bets pay out automatically.

### What pays what

- **Single number** (straight up): pays **35 to 1** — the long shot.
- **Dozens** (1–12, 13–24, 25–36) and **columns**: pay **2 to 1**.
- **Red/Black, Even/Odd, 1–18/19–36**: pay **even money** (1 to 1).
- If the ball lands on **0**, all the outside bets (color, even/odd, etc.) lose — that's the house edge.

### Good to know

- Like the rest of the arcade, you start with **$1,000**, your bank **carries over**, and the house will re-stake you if you bust.
- **♪** toggles sound, **↺** resets your bank.

### Keyboard shortcuts

`1` `2` `3` `4` — pick chip value · `U` undo · `C` clear · `R` rebet · `Enter` / `Space` spin
