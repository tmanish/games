# Games

A little arcade that runs right in your browser. No downloads, no sign-ups, no apps — open it and play. Works on desktop or phone, and even offline once the page has loaded.

## Start playing

<a href="https://tmanish.github.io/games/">Open the arcade menu -> </a> then pick a cabinet:

- `↑` / `↓` — move between games
- `Enter` — start the highlighted game
- Or just tap/click the one you want

If it's hosted online, the menu link will take you straight there.

## 🂡 Blackjack — The Velvet Ace

Classic Vegas blackjack: it's you against the dealer, and the goal is to get closer to **21** than the dealer without going over.

### New to blackjack? The basics

- Number cards are worth their value, face cards (J, Q, K) are worth 10, and an Ace is worth **1 or 11** — whichever helps you most.
- Go over 21 and you **bust** — instant loss.
- An Ace plus a 10-value card on your first two cards is a **blackjack** — the best hand, and it pays extra.

### How a round works

1. **Place your bet.** Click the chips ($5, $25, $100, $500) to stack your wager, then hit **Deal**.
   - **Clear** wipes your bet, **Rebet** repeats your last one.
2. **Play your hand.** You'll see your cards and one of the dealer's. Choose:
   - **Hit** — take another card.
   - **Stand** — keep your total and end your turn.
   - **Double** — double your bet and take exactly one more card.
   - **Split** — if your first two cards match, split them into two separate hands.
3. **The dealer plays,** then you win, lose, or tie.

### What pays what

- Win the hand: pays **even money** (bet $50, win $50).
- Natural blackjack: pays **3 to 2** (bet $50, win $75).
- Tie ("push"): your bet comes back.
- The dealer must stand on 17, so they can't keep drawing forever.

### Good to know

- You start with **$1,000** in chips, and your bank **carries over** every time you come back.
- Run out? The house will stake you another $1,000 so the night keeps going.
- **♪** toggles the casino sound on and off. **↺** resets your bank to $1,000.

### Keyboard shortcuts

`H` hit · `S` stand · `D` double · `P` split · `Enter` deal
